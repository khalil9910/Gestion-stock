Choice js

A vanilla, lightweight (~20kb gzipped 🎉), configurable select box/text input plugin. Similar to Select2 and Selectize but without the jQuery dependency.


More examples at Choice-js 

            <div class="form-floating">
                <select class="form-select js-choice" multiple>
                    <option selected>Small (W:50 H:20 L:40)</option>
                    <option>Medium (W:54 H:24 L:44)</option>
                    <option>Large (W:50 H:28 L:48)</option>
                </select>
                <label>Sizes</label>
            </div>
